Why AwaazSafe Exists

AwaazSafe is an anonymous voice-to-text reporting portal for Vizag students that bypasses biased college gatekeepers to route complaints straight to external authorities.

The Problem

Students in Visakhapatnam colleges facing online abuse and ragging lack a confidential reporting channel. Fear of biased internal committees and institutional retaliation forces them into silence. Existing portals require login, go through college admin first, or simply don't exist.

What Makes Us Different

  • 🔒 Zero-knowledge anonymity — no account, no personal data, no server logs
  • 🎙️ Voice-to-text in Telugu & English — no typing required
  • 🏛️ Direct routing to external authorities — UGC, AP Police, NCW, Vizag Cyber Cell
  • 📱 Works on any phone browser — no app download needed

How It Works

  1. Student presses mic, speaks the incident in Telugu or English
  2. Gemini AI structures the speech into a formal legal complaint
  3. Student reviews, removes identity markers, and optionally attaches evidence
  4. Report is routed to the correct external authority — not the college admin
  5. Student gets a 16-character encrypted case key to track the complaint
